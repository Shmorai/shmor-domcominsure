Before you buy from anyone

  • UK: check the firm on the FCA Financial Services Register and beware clone firms using real firms’ names.
  • US: check the insurer and agent license with your state insurance department; the NAIC links to every state.
  • EU: check with your national supervisor; EIOPA lists national authorities.

Ghost broking: cheap “insurance” sold through social media or messaging apps can be fake or based on false details, leaving you uninsured. Buy from authorised firms only.

Insurers vs brokers vs comparison sites

An insurer carries the risk and pays claims. A broker or agent arranges cover with insurers; independent brokers can approach several. A comparison site shows quotes from participating providers and passes you to them to buy. Some brands are “managing general agents” that design products underwritten by other insurers; the policy documents name the actual insurer.
