Tradesperson and contractor insurance

Tradespeople work in other people’s homes and on sites, carry valuable tools, and often take on helpers. That’s why trade insurance packages combine liability, tools and vans, with employers’ cover once you’re not working alone.

Photo: Emmanuel Ikwuegbu on Unsplash

At a glance

Ref. COM-TRADE
Core cover
Public liability (UK) / general liability (US), usually £1m–£10m or $1m+.
Usually added
Tools and equipment, contract works, products liability.
Legal must-haves
Employers’ liability (UK) or workers’ comp (US) if you have employees; motor cover for vans.
Often required
Main contractors may ask for specific limits and certificates before you start on site.
Watch out for
Tools left in vans overnight, heat work (welding, roofing torches), work at height limits.

Trade insurance at a glance

Usually covered

  • Injury to the public and damage to clients’ property
  • Tools and equipment (if added), including theft from a locked van on many policies
  • Contract works: damage to the work in progress
  • Employers’ liability for staff and some labour-only subcontractors

Usually not covered

  • Faulty workmanship itself (the cost of putting it right)
  • Tools left in a van overnight without meeting conditions
  • Heat work unless declared
  • Work above stated heights or depths

Common add-ons

  • Tools cover
  • Contract works
  • Hired-in plant
  • Personal accident
  • Legal expenses

General guide only. Cover varies by insurer and policy: the policy wording and key facts document decide what you’re actually insured for.

Which trades this covers

Builders, electricians, plumbers, gas engineers, roofers, plasterers, painters and decorators, carpenters, landscapers, cleaners, handymen and more. Insurers price by trade because risks differ: roofing and work with heat carry more risk than decorating. Declare every type of work you do.

Tools and vans

Tools are expensive and theft from vans is a common claim. Check the conditions: some policies require tools to be removed from vehicles overnight or kept in a locked steel cabinet, and may only pay for visible forced entry. Your van needs its own van insurance with the right class of use.

Find work and compare tradespeople

Homeowners looking for trusted local tradespeople can compare options on our sister sites TradesStation and LocalPlumbers. Insured, accredited trades are easier to recommend.

Frequently asked questions

Is public liability insurance compulsory for tradespeople?
Not by law, but many clients, letting agents and main contractors require it, and a single claim can be expensive.
Do I need employers’ liability for a labourer I pay by the day?
Often yes. Labour-only subcontractors and casual helpers can count as employees for employers’ liability purposes in the UK. Check with your insurer.
Does my policy cover putting right my own bad work?
Usually no. It covers resulting damage to other property, not the cost of redoing faulty work.
