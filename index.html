How to compare insurance properly

  1. Know what you need

    Start with legal and contract requirements, then the risks that would really hurt.

  2. Get the sums right

    Rebuild costs, replacement values, liability limits and indemnity periods drive everything else.

  3. Compare like for like

    Same cover, excess and extras on every quote, from comparison sites, direct insurers and brokers.

  4. Read the key facts

    Check exclusions, limits and conditions in the key facts or IPID before you buy.

  5. Check who you’re buying from

    Use the FCA register (UK), your state insurance department (US) or national regulator (EU).

Domestic and commercial, side by side

Most insurance sites focus on either households or businesses. Real life mixes them: you might own a home, let out a flat, run a business from the spare room and drive a van for work. Frequently asked questions

Is DomCom Insure an insurer or a broker?
Neither. We publish independent guides and point you to regulated insurers, brokers and comparison services. Always check a provider’s authorisation before buying.
What’s the difference between domestic and commercial insurance?
Domestic (personal lines) insurance protects individuals and households: home, contents, car, travel and life. Commercial insurance protects businesses: liability, employees, premises, professional work, vehicles and data.
Which insurance is compulsory?
In most places, motor insurance for vehicles on the road, and employers’ liability or workers’ comp once you employ staff. Lenders, landlords and clients add requirements such as buildings cover or public liability. See the table above or try the cover checker.
